- A simulator of public men's bathrooms using cooperative A* for multi agent pathfinding.
- Agents have a sequence of tasks that they complete.
- Urinals are chosen by agents based on cleanliness, social comfort, wall proximity, and distance from the entrance.
- Agents may choose to skip flushing or washing their hands.
- Custom map making using text files.
